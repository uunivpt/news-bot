# Ethical search discovery and distribution — PoliticsHub

## Purpose
Use original reporting, primary documents, explainers, internal topic links and transparent cross-platform distribution to help readers find relevant work. Avoid site-reputation abuse, paid placement for ranking manipulation, mass-produced near-duplicates and exaggerated promises of search ranking.

## Editorial workflow
1. Identify an enduring reader question or useful news context; ensure the article contributes original explanation rather than a lightly rewritten source summary.
2. Document primary records, dates, jurisdiction and uncertainty. Keep publisher attribution and corrections policy visible.
3. Publish at a stable canonical URL with meaningful title/description and (where accurate) Article schema. Confirm the page is accessible before promoting.
4. Add relevant internal links between source news, topic hubs, guides and official records. Don't mass link irrelevant posts.
5. Compose a genuinely helpful excerpt through /editorial-share-kit.html for LinkedIn or Medium, pointing back to the canonical original. Publishing requires a human account holder; no automated third-party submissions.
6. Review Google Search Console performance (indexing, impressions, clicks, queries) and separate actual indexing from assumptions about ranking.
7. Check the page's own editorial originality and indexing eligibility before adding it to search sitemaps. Do not bypass existing thin-content eligibility controls.

## Guardrails
- Do not republish full third-party articles or remove attribution.
- Do not misrepresent other publishers' platforms as editorial endorsements.
- Do not keyword-stuff or post near-identical copies across platforms.
- Do not expect backlinks or third-party pages to guarantee PoliticsHub search rankings.
- This share kit is client-side and does not authenticate with LinkedIn, Medium or Google.
