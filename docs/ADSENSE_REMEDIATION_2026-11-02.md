# AdSense remediation — 2 November 2026

## Evidence and scope

The 2 November AdSense email is a generic application rejection. It does not identify the specific policy violation. No account-level rejection reason has been retrieved from the authenticated AdSense dashboard. This change does not claim Google approval or full compliance.

Official references reviewed:
- https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/10502938
- https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724

Relevant requirements include accurate representation, intellectual property rights, original useful content, and no ads on low-value, replicated or utility screens. Attribution alone does not establish permission or original value.

## Changes

Two standalone reports bypassed the normal current-copy editorial advertising gate. They carried ad scripts directly and presented claims/citations without a verifiable review record in the application. Their original text remains recoverable in Git history. Both public URLs now show dated, ad-free, noindex review notices. The notices do not assert that the underlying claims are false. Neither report should be reinstated until accessible sources support its claims and an editor reviews the final copy.

Removed the hard-coded verified/breaking report metadata, homepage/category promotion and standalone news-sitemap entries. Added a public correction-log entry and clarified editorial standards for evidence, original value, rights and sensitive material. Existing source-derived briefs remain ad-free under the existing runtime review gate.
