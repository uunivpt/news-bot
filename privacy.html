Last updated: September 19, 2026

Depending on how you use the service, we may process information such as account identifiers, public profile information, content and media that you choose to publish through an integration, technical information needed to operate the service, and information contained in requests or messages you send to us.

If you connect an Instagram professional account to PoliticsHub, we may process the information and permissions made available to us through the applicable Meta/Instagram APIs. We use this information only to provide the features you request, such as publishing or managing content and maintaining the integration.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us host, operate, secure, or deliver the service, and by third-party platforms when you explicitly use an integration with those platforms.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of information associated with your use of the service where applicable.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

You may disconnect an integration or request deletion of information associated with your use of PoliticsHub, subject to information we may be required to retain by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Children's privacy

PoliticsHub is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children who are below the minimum age required by applicable law to use the service.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when material updates are made.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please use the contact method provided by the PoliticsHub service or the contact information associated with the PoliticsHub Meta application.
