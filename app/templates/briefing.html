How this briefing is selected

We take the newest available report from each desk, then fill remaining places by publication time, up to six reports. Filters apply to the latest 200 available published reports for your chosen desk, so this is not an exhaustive archive search.

The feed may use a saved snapshot during an outage. Dates stay attached to their reports; opening this page does not make an older report new. Source links identify the originating report and do not imply independent verification of every claim.
