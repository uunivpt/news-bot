Nana Patekar dies at 75 in Goa, leaving a lasting cinema legacy

The acclaimed actor, celebrated for his distinctive performances across Hindi and Marathi cinema, died on 8 October 2026.

8 October 2026, 06:00 ISTGoa, IndiaPoliticsHub News DeskSources: Reuters, AP

Veteran actor Nana Patekar died in Goa on Thursday, 8 October 2026. He was 75. Reuters and the Associated Press confirmed the news, which prompted tributes from colleagues, public figures and film audiences.

Over a career spanning nearly five decades, Patekar built a reputation for his commanding screen presence, distinctive delivery and memorable characters. His work connected audiences across Hindi and Marathi cinema.

A career across languages and generations

His celebrated films included Parinda, Krantiveer and Ab Tak Chhappan. The Marathi drama Natsamrat further demonstrated the range of his performances. He received three National Film Awards and was honoured with the Padma Shri.

Published reports said he suffered a cardiac arrest in Goa. Prime Minister Narendra Modi and members of the film community were among those who paid tribute to his legacy.

For generations of viewers, his performances and contribution to theatre and film will remain an important part of Indian cinema.
