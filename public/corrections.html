Corrections Policy

How PoliticsHub.in handles factual errors, updates and reader correction requests.

Corrections

If we publish a material factual error, we will correct the article and, where appropriate, make the correction clear to readers. We do not silently change material facts to make an old story appear newly reported.

Request a correction

Email news@politicshub.in with the article URL, the disputed statement and reliable supporting evidence.

Updates

Substantive updates may be reflected in the article's modified date. Original publication dates are not artificially refreshed.

Independent reporting. Clearly.
