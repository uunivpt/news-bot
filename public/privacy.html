How PoliticsHub.in handles personal data and browser information.

Last updated: 7 October 2026.

PoliticsHub.in is a source-linked news website. We collect only the information needed to operate the site, deliver requested features and protect the service.

Legal basis and consent

Where consent is required, we ask before using optional browser storage or adding an email address to the newsletter. You can withdraw newsletter consent by unsubscribing, and you can change browser-storage choices from the Cookie Policy.

Children and teenagers

The news website can be read without creating an account. To avoid collecting children's personal data through our public newsletter, newsletter signup is currently limited to people aged 18 or older. Users under 18 should not submit personal data through the newsletter form.

Who receives data

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical request data to deliver and secure the site. Email enquiries may be processed by our email provider. We do not sell personal data. We do not currently load advertising or analytics tags on the public site; advertising remains disabled until appropriate consent-management requirements are configured.

International processing

Some infrastructure providers may process data in countries outside your own. Where applicable, we rely on the provider's contractual and legal transfer mechanisms.

Your choices and rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of personal data, withdraw newsletter consent, or raise a privacy question. See Data Rights for the request methods.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures such as HTTPS, restricted administrator sessions and access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service, law or third-party providers change. The latest version and update date will be posted here.
