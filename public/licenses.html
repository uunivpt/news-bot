Software, fonts, media and source-content rules used by PoliticsHub.in.

PoliticsHub assets

The PoliticsHub.in name, logo, favicon and original interface artwork are treated as PoliticsHub assets unless a separate notice says otherwise. Do not assume that third-party news photographs or publisher logos are owned by PoliticsHub.in.

News photographs and video

PoliticsHub.in does not intentionally claim ownership of source-publisher media. Public website images are restricted to media with an explicit compatible license; unknown-license source images are not intentionally published by the website. Automated Reel image search is restricted to public-domain/CC0-style results where possible, with provenance retained.

Openverse

Openverse is a discovery service, not a guarantee that every indexed license is correct. We retain the creator/provider, license metadata and source landing page where available and avoid treating search results as proof of ownership.

Fonts

Inter, Bricolage Grotesque and Barlow Condensed are used under their applicable open font licenses as distributed by their upstream providers. Font files are not offered for separate redistribution by PoliticsHub.in.
