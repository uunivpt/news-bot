About Us

PoliticsHub.in is a source-linked digital newsroom covering politics, public affairs, India and global developments.

Who we are

PoliticsHub.in publishes timely source-linked briefs with clear attribution and, where multiple sources are available, cross-source comparison and context. Our focus is on making important public-affairs developments easier to understand.

Ownership & publisher

Publisher: PoliticsHub.in. The publication uses automated source collection and deterministic processing, with source attribution retained where available. Editorial contact: politicshub.in@gmail.com.

How stories are produced

Our newsroom system collects public-source material, normalizes it, checks for duplicate coverage and applies deterministic processing. We do not describe source-derived material as independently reported unless it has been independently reported and verified. Source links are retained where available. Automated processing does not change our obligation to correct material errors.
