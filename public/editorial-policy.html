Editorial Policy

Our editorial principles for sourcing, attribution, corrections and automated newsroom processing.

Accuracy first

We aim to distinguish verified facts, attributed claims and analysis. We link to original sources where available and avoid presenting a source's claims as independently verified facts when they are not.

Attribution

Source names and source links are displayed where available. Headlines and summaries should not remove important context from the underlying report.

Automation

PoliticsHub.in uses software to collect, normalize, deduplicate and format newsroom material. Automated processing is deterministic and does not replace the requirement to provide source attribution.

Conflicts

Paid or sponsored material must be clearly identified and must not be presented as independent editorial reporting.
