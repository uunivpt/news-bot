Cookie & Storage Policy

What browser storage is used and how to control it.

Last updated: 7 October 2026.

Current use

TechnologyPurposeOptional?
Privacy-choice storageRemembers whether you selected essential-only or allowed preferences.Necessary to remember the choice.
Local storage for theme and recent-story cacheRemembers appearance and speeds repeat visits.Yes. Used only after you choose “Allow preferences”.
Administrator session cookieSecures signed-in newsroom/admin sessions. It is not used for ordinary visitors.Necessary for authenticated administrators.
Service worker/browser cacheCaches core site files for performance and resilience.Used for site delivery; you can clear it in browser site-data settings.

Advertising and analytics

Advertising and analytics tags are currently disabled on the public site. Before Google advertising is enabled for users in regions where consent is required, PoliticsHub.in must use an appropriate consent-management setup; for personalized Google ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google requires a certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF.

Third-party resources

The site may request web fonts or other technical resources from third-party providers. Those requests can reveal normal connection information such as IP address and browser details even when no advertising cookie is used.

Your choice

You can also clear PoliticsHub.in site data using your browser settings. Selecting essential-only removes PoliticsHub optional local-storage preferences on this device.
