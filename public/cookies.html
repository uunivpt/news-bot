Advertising and analytics

News pages load Google AdSense, which may use cookies and similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement and personalization. Advertising availability and privacy messages are managed through Google AdSense. Our “Essential only” and “Allow preferences” buttons control PoliticsHub.in local-storage preferences; they do not grant or withdraw advertising consent. You can manage Google advertising preferences at My Ad Center and block or clear cookies in your browser. Where an advertising consent message is shown, use that message to manage your advertising choices. We do not currently load separate analytics tags.

Third-party resources

News pages request the Google AdSense script from pagead2.googlesyndication.com. Some pages also request Google Fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. These services may receive your IP address and browser information. See Google’s partner-site privacy information. Links to external news sources open third-party websites under their own cookie and privacy practices.

Your choice

You can also clear PoliticsHub.in site data using your browser settings. Selecting essential-only removes PoliticsHub optional local-storage preferences on this device.
